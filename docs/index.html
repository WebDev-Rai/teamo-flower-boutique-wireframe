Current planning language uses a 48-hour notice period. Te’Amo will approve the final rule and any holiday exceptions.

Can I request a specific flower color?

Natural and tinted color availability, added costs, and substitution rules will appear here after approval.

Do you offer delivery and pickup?

Delivery area, fees, available windows, and a safe public pickup process are still being defined.
